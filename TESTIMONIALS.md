# Collecting Testimonials

The homepage has a **Love Notes** section for short quotes from past couples and families. It stays hidden until the first quote is added, so there's never an empty section on the site.

Each card shows the guests' names, the event and month, their words, and where the words came from. Under the cards, the section says "Selected testimonials, shared with our guests' permission" and links to the full Google reviews.

## The rules (keep it honest and legal)

The FTC's review rule (in force since October 2024) carries real fines, so:

- **Real words only.** Never write or "improve" a testimonial. Trimming with "…" is fine; rewording is not.
- **Get written permission first.** A reply email saying "yes, you can use it" is enough. Keep that email. This applies even to a review already posted on Google or The Knot.
- **No paying for praise.** Don't offer a discount or gift for a _positive_ review. If you thank people for leaving _any_ review, that's fine.
- **Stars only from real ratings.** Add a star rating only when quoting a review that actually had stars (Google, The Knot). Quotes from emails or texts show no stars.

## When to ask

The best time is 1–3 weeks after the event, while the day is still fresh. Past couples from the last year or two are worth asking too.

## Email to send (copy and adjust)

> **Subject:** A small favor from The Barn at Sunset Farm
>
> Hi [names],
>
> It was such a joy hosting your [wedding / celebration] in [month]. We'd love to share a few of your words on our website to help other couples picture their day here.
>
> Would you write us 2–3 sentences about your experience? Anything is welcome: the views, the barn, the cottage, or how the day felt.
>
> Please also tell us:
>
> 1. How you'd like your names shown (for example "Sarah & James").
> 2. That it's OK for us to post your words on our website.
>
> If you have a moment, we'd also be grateful for a Google review: [your Google review link]
>
> Thank you again for celebrating with us!

**To get your Google review link:** search Google for "The Barn at Sunset Farm" while signed in to the business account. Click **Ask for reviews** (sometimes called **Get more reviews**) and copy the link.

## Adding a testimonial to the website

Send the quote and the details below to whoever updates the website. It's a two-minute change in `lib/testimonials.ts`.

| Detail                                | Example                                       |
| ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Names, as they want them shown        | Sarah & James                                 |
| Event                                 | Wedding                                       |
| Month and year                        | June 2026                                     |
| Their words                           | (copied exactly)                              |
| Where it came from                    | Email to us / Google review / The Knot review |
| Star rating, if it was a rated review | 5                                             |
| Permission received?                  | Yes (reply email saved)                       |

Three to six testimonials is the sweet spot. Rotate in fresh ones each season so the newest dates stay recent.
