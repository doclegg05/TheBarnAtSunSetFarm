# Full-page film: shot list (draft for Britt's approval)

One continuous, AI-rendered camera journey behind the whole `/experience`
page, from first light to a starry night. It replaces the 3D background.
Each chapter starts on the exact last frame of the one before, so the film
never cuts. Venue and setting only: no people.

The page already moves through six times of day; each chapter matches one.

| #   | Chapter                | Time of day | Behind these sections        | Length |
| --- | ---------------------- | ----------- | ---------------------------- | ------ |
| 0   | The descent (done)     | Dawn        | Arrive, opening title        | 18 s   |
| 1   | The porch wakes up     | Morning     | Our story, The day           | ~12 s  |
| 2   | The hilltop lawn       | Golden hour | The barn, Amenities          | ~12 s  |
| 3   | Barn in the warm light | Sunset      | Stay the night, Packages     | ~12 s  |
| 4   | Lights on              | Dusk        | Planning, Explore the region | ~12 s  |
| 5   | Stars over the hilltop | Night       | Questions, Inquire, Finale   | ~12 s  |

Total: about 75 seconds of film, streamed one chapter at a time.

## The shots

**1. The porch wakes up (morning).** Picks up at the barn doors where the
descent ends. The camera drifts slowly left along the long covered porch on the barn's side.
Sun breaks over the ridge, mist lifts out of the valley, and dew catches the
light on the lawn. Calm, slow movement, because the longest text sits here.

**2. The hilltop lawn (golden hour).** The camera rises a little and glides
out across the mown lawn toward the edge of the hilltop, turning to look
back at the barn with the layered ridges behind it. Long golden shadows.
_To confirm:_ where ceremonies actually happen on the property, so the
camera lingers there and no feature that doesn't exist is shown.

**3. Barn in the warm light (sunset).** A slow arc around the front of the
barn as the sun drops toward the ridges. Light flares through the treeline,
the cedar doors stand open and the inside glows warm.

**4. Lights on (dusk).** Purple-orange sky, mountains fading to blue. The
porch string lights come on, then the camera eases back from the porch to
take in the whole barn.

**5. Stars over the hilltop (night).** The camera rises slowly up and away.
The barn's windows glow under a sky full of stars, with fireflies over the
lawn. It ends on a wide, still frame that sits quietly behind the inquiry
form and finale.

## The look

- One colour grade across every chapter, light film grain, a soft vignette.
- Text sections get a subtle shaded backing so copy stays crisp over motion.
- Chapters behind long text move slowly; the big moments move more.
- Labelled "Artistic rendering", like the descent.

## How each chapter is made

1. Two or three still keyframes for the chapter, generated from the real
   venue photos so the barn stays accurate, with the lawn finished green.
2. Video clips chained start-frame to start-frame (Veo 3.1). The descent
   showed that forcing a clip to land on a set end frame causes dissolves,
   so end frames are used only where the move is gentle.
3. Each chapter is checked frame by frame for dissolves, warping and jumps
   before Britt sees it.
