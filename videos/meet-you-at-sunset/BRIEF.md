## Intent

A promo for The Barn at Sunset Farm to run on the website and social media and
spark inquiries. Concept chosen from the pitch round: **Meet You at Sunset**. One
wedding day told by the light, with a small clock that ticks from 3:00 PM to
8:30 PM while the color warms scene by scene. It closes on the dusk barn and
"Your forever starts here." Britt asked for it to be "creative and fun."
